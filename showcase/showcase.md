# Shatter

Shatter is a fast, tense rock shooter set in a field wrapped edge to edge. Fly by turning and thrusting, carry your momentum, and keep moving as the rocks close in.

Clear each wave by breaking every rock down through its fragments. Large and medium rocks have armor, so keep firing until their damaged shells finally split. A hunting saucer crosses the field on its own cadence and takes aim at your ship.

The star at the centre changes every shot. It pulls bullets and rocks into curved paths while leaving your ship under your control. Learn to send a shot around the star and into a rock hiding on its far side; rocks swallowed by the core return from the edge.

Your torpedo is a single guided shot that flies true through the well and destroys any rock outright. Fire it when a target is lined up, then survive the ten seconds it takes to recharge.
